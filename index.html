01 — The rail

Four ways to
put it in a ceiling.

Same 48V bus, same fixtures. What changes is how the rail meets the plasterboard: sitting on it, buried flush in it, or hanging below it.

02 — Control

Every head is
addressable.

Dim the whole circuit, or speak to one fixture. The M7 accepts three control languages so the system fits the building's existing wiring rather than dictating it.

PWM

Circuit-level dimming through conventional wall devices. Compatible with phase dimming and DALI dimming on the driver side. The simplest retrofit path.

DALI-2

Individual digital addressing of each fixture on the run. Standards-based, so the ceiling integrates with the wider building management system without a proprietary bridge.

Casambi

Bluetooth mesh control from a phone or tablet on iOS and Android. Group fixtures, build scenes and schedule programs without pulling a control cable.

Beam diameter is geometry, not marketing. Move the sliders to size a beam against a real ceiling height, and read the on-centre spacing that gives you an even wash.

Spacing on centre—

Diameter is the 50% intensity cone at the working plane. Spacing assumes overlapping beam edges for an even wash — tighten it for grazing, widen it for accent. Request the IES or LDT file for certified photometry.
